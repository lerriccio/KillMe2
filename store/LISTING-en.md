# KillMe2: Forge Survivors

Short description (under 80 characters):

Four heroes, raider hordes and giant bosses in an offline arcade roguelite.

## Full description

An impossible signal awakens four heroes. Choose Axiom, Vulkor, Bastion or Nyx and fight your way through raiders, crystals and special attacks in KillMe2: Forge Survivors.

Move, dodge and choose your upgrades: aiming and attacking are automatic. Each hero has a distinct weapon and super, from piercing railguns and a seismic axe to an explosive howitzer and bouncing chakrams.

• Ten campaign missions across two chapters: the Canyon and the Mushroom Forest.
• Decode signals, destroy relays, defend a generator, escort a drone and recover memories.
• Twelve-wave Arcade mode with three bosses.
• Endless Boss mode with increasing difficulty.
• Twenty-four upgrades with ranks and rarities to build your own combination.
• A daily challenge with shared starting conditions and on-device personal scores, without an online leaderboard.
• Earn hero mastery, cosmetic appearances and weapon variants through play.
• Guided training, adjustable touch controls and options to reduce visual effects and screen motion.
• Resume your run, export a backup and import your progress.
• English and Italian.

Play offline without accounts, advertising or in-app purchases. Saves remain on your device: export a backup before uninstalling.

Original pixel art, a team to discover and one more wave to survive. How long can you last?
