Italiano

Puoi cancellare i progressi nelle impostazioni del gioco, con conferma. Lingua e preferenze possono essere rimosse anche cancellando i dati dell’app nelle impostazioni Android. Non esistono account da eliminare.

I collegamenti alle licenze e agli autori nei riconoscimenti aprono siti esterni nel browser; tali siti possono trattare dati secondo le rispettive informative. Anche Google Play e Android possono trattare dati di distribuzione e diagnostica secondo le loro informative: non si tratta di analisi integrate nel gioco.

English

This policy covers the offline Android version of KillMe2: Forge Survivors, developed by Lerriccio. Current contact: leoriccio@gmail.com. The owner’s final identity and contact details must be verified before Google Play publication.

The game does not require an account or send data to developer servers. It has no advertising SDKs, usage analytics, in-app purchases or online leaderboards. It does not request access to location, contacts, microphone, camera or advertising identifiers.

Preferences, language, records, campaign progress, hero mastery, daily scores and your current run are stored in the app’s local storage until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall. Android automatic backup is disabled.

You may voluntarily export a JSON file and choose where to save it using the system document picker, or import a file you select. These files are not sent to the developer. The service or location you choose to store them may have its own terms and privacy policy.

You can delete progress in game settings after confirmation. Language and preferences can also be removed by clearing app data in Android settings. There are no user accounts to delete.

Credit and license links open external sites in your browser, governed by their own privacy policies. Google Play and Android may separately process distribution and diagnostic data under their own policies; this is not analytics embedded in the game.

The game has no chat or user-uploaded content. The intended audience and content rating will appear in the final Google Play listing. Future changes in data practices require updates to this policy and the store declarations.
